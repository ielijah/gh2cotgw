Исходный результат: [title-logo-source.png](title-logo-source.png).
Готовые индексированные данные для ROM: title-logo-indexed.bin. Импорт: tools/import_title.ps1.

## Запрос

Use case: text-localization. Asset: Cyrillic replacement title logo for the Sega Genesis game in the reference. Reference image is the ORIGINAL style to reproduce closely. Produce ONLY the two-line title logo on pure black, no subtitle, no copyright, no frame, no extra graphics. Text EXACTLY: upper line 'Чингис', lower line 'ХАН II'. Upper line: expressive flowing connected calligraphic italic handwriting matching original Genghis, thick and thin red brush strokes, blue hard offset edging; must be natural Cyrillic calligraphy, not thin stick handwriting. Lower line: enormous heavy condensed geometric sans-serif block capitals, exactly matching original KHAN II visual weight and angular cuts, purple/lavender vertical highlights, dark purple hard extruded shadow. Absolutely no slab serifs or Roman serif letters. Upper calligraphy overlaps upper edge of lower capitals as in reference. Lower capitals fill width, II on right integrated at same height. Maintain original early-1990s 16-bit pixel-art character: crisp pixel steps and restrained palette, no smooth 3D, no glow, no texture. Wide composition, logo occupancy approximately 90% width and 80% height, aspect about 2.2:1. This will be reduced to 192x88 native pixels; retain bold readable forms and no fine decoration.
